research, documentation, accountability, verification, and archival purposes.

logos, or other material visible in a capture.

Metadata authored by FreeTheDotYE may be reused with attribution to
FreeTheDotYE and a link to this repository. Reusers are responsible for
assessing the copyright, privacy, evidentiary, and legal implications of any
third-party content in a screenshot.

A screenshot is an observation from one external network vantage on one day.
It should not be represented as proof of continuous availability, global
reachability, registrant identity, or ownership beyond what the associated
record and cited evidence establish.
